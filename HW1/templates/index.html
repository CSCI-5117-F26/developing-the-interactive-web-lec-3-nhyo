Student Eating Habits Survey

This short survey is about what students usually eat and their everyday eating habits. We are interested in learning about students' favorite foods, where they usually get their meals, and their dietary preferences.

The survey will take about 1–2 minutes to complete. It does not ask for your name or contact information.
